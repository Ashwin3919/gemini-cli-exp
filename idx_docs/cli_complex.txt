  ---

  Detailed Module Breakdown

   * src/core/ (box.py, filters.py, ops.py)
       * Internal Independence: These modules are the foundational building blocks. They do not import from any other project
         directories.
       * Usage: They provide the grid geometry (Box), Fourier filters (Filter), and numerical operations (md_cic_nd,
         gradient_2nd_order) used throughout the physics engine and analysis tools.

   * src/physics/ (cosmology.py, initial_conds.py, system.py)
       * Dependencies: 
           * initial_conds.py and system.py import numerical kernels from src/core/ops.py and src/core/filters.py.
           * They both depend on src/solver/state.py for the State and HamiltonianSystem abstractions.
       * Note: cosmology.py is independent of other project modules.

   * src/solver/ (integrator.py, state.py)
       * Dependencies: integrator.py depends on state.py for type definitions.
       * Usage: Provides the integration logic used by main.py to advance the PoissonVlasov system defined in src/physics/.

   * src/utils/ (analysis.py, config_parser.py, io.py, plotting.py)
       * Dependencies: plotting.py imports md_cic_nd from src/core/ops.py to generate density fields for visualization.
       * Note: analysis.py, config_parser.py, and io.py are largely independent of other source modules, though they are heavily used
         by the top-level script.
